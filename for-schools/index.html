Common Questions

Frequently asked

What grades do you cover?

Grades 1–9, structured as one skill ladder across three stages: Explore (1–3), Build (4–6), and Create (7–9), across four tracks — Electronics & Robotics, Coding & Apps, Animation & Design, and 3D Design.

Do we need to buy hardware before we start?

No. The curriculum is built simulator-first and free-tier-first, so teaching can begin right away. When a school is ready for physical builds, we bring in Blix, an Indian STEM hardware supplier, to keep components affordable.

Who actually teaches the sessions?

A teacher already on your staff. We train them to run sessions day-to-day and visit regularly to coach and troubleshoot — we're not sending in an outside instructor indefinitely.

What kind of schools is this built for?

International schools wanting a modern, hands-on STEM track, and schools in tier 2/3 cities wanting an affordable, simulator-first entry point into robotics, coding, and design.

Where are you based?

Hyderabad, Telangana — and we currently work with schools across the Hyderabad region.

How do we get started?

Contact us or read how partnering works. We start with an assessment of your school's space, grade levels, and goals.
