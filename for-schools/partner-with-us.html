For Schools / Partner With Us

Built to run without us in the room.

Most programmes need an outside instructor to show up forever. Ours doesn't. We set up your curriculum and lab, train a teacher already on your staff to run it day-to-day, and visit regularly to coach and troubleshoot — so the programme is genuinely yours, not something you keep renting.

What's included

  • A grade-appropriate curriculum slice (Grades 1–9, any subset)
  • Lab/makerspace planning and hardware sourcing
  • Teacher training and ongoing visits
  • Access to video and lesson materials your teacher can reuse

Who this is for

International schools wanting a modern, hands-on STEM track, and schools in tier 2/3 cities wanting an affordable, simulator-first entry point into robotics, coding, and design.
