What We Do

Three things, working together.

A curriculum alone gathers dust without a lab. A lab alone sits idle without a trained teacher. We don't sell one piece — we set up all three, so a school can actually run this on its own once we've helped build it.

Curriculum & Content

A grade-by-grade skill ladder for Grades 1–9, covering electronics and robotics, coding and app development, animation, and 3D design. Built simulator-first and free-tier-first, so a school can start teaching before spending on hardware — and delivered as structured video and lesson content your teacher can pick up and run.

More on Curriculum & Content →

Lab & Makerspace Setup

We help schools plan and set up the physical space — from a single classroom corner to a dedicated makerspace — including sourcing affordable, reliable hardware (we work with Blix, an Indian STEM hardware supplier, for low-cost individual components).

More on Lab & Makerspace Setup →

Teacher Training & Support

This is the part most vendors skip. Rather than sending in outside instructors indefinitely, we train a teacher already on your staff to run sessions day-to-day using our curriculum and materials, and we stay involved — visiting periodically to observe, coach, and troubleshoot — so the programme keeps running well after we've handed it over.
