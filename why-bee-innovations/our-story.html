Our Story

Bee Innovations was founded by Devendhar Bachhu, who came to education through engineering, not away from it.

After working as a design engineer in concrete 3D printing, he moved into robotics mentoring at Atal Tinkering Labs, then into curriculum and program management at Creya Learning, before becoming a Makerspace facilitator and IB MYP Design teacher.

Bee Innovations grew out of a simple observation from that path: schools weren't short on curious kids — they were short on the bridge between a child's curiosity and something they could actually build.
